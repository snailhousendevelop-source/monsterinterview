snailhouseNdevelop (달팽이집개발) · Effective date: November 17, 2026

This English version is a translation for convenience. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

snailhouseNdevelop ("we", "the developer") respects the privacy of everyone who plays the mobile game "Monster Interviewer" ("the Game") and complies with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea.
Summary: The Game has no account sign-up. Your game progress is stored on your device and, if you sign in to Google Play Games, is also backed up to your Google account storage. Google processes device information, advertising IDs, app usage data and similar data for ads (Google AdMob), payments (Google Play Billing), and usage statistics and crash analysis (Google Firebase). We do not run our own servers.

2. Retention and deletion

  • Game progress: stored on your device and deleted when you uninstall the Game. (If device backup is on, a copy may remain in your Google account backup, which you can delete in your device settings.)
  • Cloud Save: kept until you delete it with "Delete Cloud Save" in the Game's settings or in your Google account settings. After deleting, it is not saved again on that device until you tap "Re-enable Cloud Save".
  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): automatically deleted 2 months after collection.
  • Crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics): automatically deleted 90 days after collection.
  • Ad and payment data: retained according to Google's retention policies.
  • Inquiry emails: kept for 1 year after we reply, then deleted. Records that Korean e-commerce law requires us to keep (payment and supply records: 5 years; consumer complaint and dispute records: 3 years) are kept for those periods.
  • Method: electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

5. Advertising ID and personalized ads

  • The Game uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads. AdMob may use your advertising ID to show ads based on your interests.
  • You can choose whether to allow personalized ads on the consent screen shown when required by law (for example, in the EEA and the UK). You can change this later under Settings > Ad Privacy Settings in the Game, where available.
  • Opting out: on Android, go to Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID.

5-2. Turning off usage statistics

  • Turn off Settings > Privacy and Terms > Share Usage Data in the Game to stop Firebase Analytics collection.
  • Crash logs (Crashlytics) are sent only when the app crashes and are used only to fix errors.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time. Contact us at the address below and we will respond without delay. See Data Deletion for details. You can delete game progress by uninstalling the Game, and Cloud Save with "Delete Cloud Save" in the Game's settings. When requesting deletion of usage statistics, please include the Support ID shown in the Game's settings. You can also manage information processed by Google in your Google account settings. Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, the UK or certain US states), you may have additional rights under local law; we will honor such requests.

7. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 (or under the age required by your local law).

8. Security

We do not run our own servers, and all information is sent to Google over encrypted connections (TLS). The Firebase console and our support mailbox are accessible only from developer accounts protected by two-step verification. Usage statistics are viewed only in aggregate, not to identify individuals.
