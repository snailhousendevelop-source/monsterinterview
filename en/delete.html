Monster Interviewer — Data Deletion

App: Monster Interviewer · Developer: snailhouseNdevelop (달팽이집개발)

Monster Interviewer has no account sign-up. Your game progress is stored on your device and, if you sign in to Google Play Games, also backed up to your Google account storage. We do not run our own servers. You can delete your data or request deletion as follows.

How to delete your data

  1. Delete game progress: Uninstall Monster Interviewer from your phone to delete the game progress stored on the device. (If device backup is on, you can also delete the app backup in Settings > Google > Backup.)
  2. Delete Cloud Save: Tap Settings > Privacy and Terms > Delete Cloud Save in the Game to delete the progress backed up to Play Games. After deleting, nothing is uploaded from that device until you tap "Re-enable Cloud Save", and the progress on the device stays as it is. (If you have already uninstalled the app, you can delete this game's data in the Play Games app under Settings > Account and privacy > Delete Play Games account and data.)
  3. Turn off usage statistics: Turn off Settings > Privacy and Terms > Share Usage Data in the Game to stop future collection. Statistics already collected are deleted automatically after 2 months; to delete them sooner, email us with your Support ID (shown in the Game's settings).
  4. Delete or reset your advertising ID: On your phone, go to Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads.
  5. Email a deletion request: To ask us to delete other data, send the following to snailhousendevelop@gmail.com.
    • Subject: Monster Interviewer data deletion request
    • Body: the data you want deleted (e.g., usage statistics, inquiry records), your Support ID from the Game's settings, and an email address for our reply
    We will reply with the result within 10 days of receiving your request.
